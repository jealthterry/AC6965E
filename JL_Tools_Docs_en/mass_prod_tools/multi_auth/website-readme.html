4.4. Using the Jieli Server

4.4.1. Account registration

In a browser, open https://fmfile.jieliapp.com/user/register to register an account

Important

  • How do I get an invitation code?

  • If you are an algorithm provider, apply by sending an email directly to Jieli at jlkf@zh-jieli.com.
    Email subject: Multi-algorithm authorization application - xx Company (in Chinese: 多算法授权申请-xx公司)
    Use the full company name
    Body:
    1. Role: algorithm provider
    2. Company introduction: e.g., our company is an algorithm company focusing on the R&D of xxxx; our customers include xxxx, xxxx, xxxx
    3. Agent number and name: e.g., 128 Jieli Technology
    4. Chip name: e.g., JL7006D
    5. Solution type: e.g., speakers, earphones, etc.
    6. Usage scenario: e.g., we are cooperating with xx Company, our company is responsible for xxx and the other company for xxx, and multi-algorithm authorization is needed to solve the xxx problem

  • If you are a manufacturer, apply through your algorithm provider, who sends an email directly to Jieli at weiyushu@zh-jieli.com.
    Email subject: Multi-algorithm authorization application - xx Company applies for an invitation code on behalf of xx Company (in Chinese: 多算法授权申请-xx公司帮助xx公司申请邀请码)
    Use the full company names
    Body:
    1. Role: manufacturer
    2. Introduction of the solution company: e.g., the manufacturer using our algorithm is xx Company, which focuses on the R&D of xxxx; its customers include xxxx, xxxx, xxxx
    3. Chip name: e.g., JL7006D
    4. Solution type: e.g., speakers, earphones, etc.
    5. Usage scenario: e.g., xx Company is cooperating with our company and needs multi-algorithm authorization to solve the xxx problem
    6. Manufacturer email address: the email address of the contact person at xx Company

4.4.2. User login

In a browser, open https://fmfile.jieliapp.com/user/login to log in to your account

4.4.3. User home page

After logging in, you are automatically redirected to https://fmfile.jieliapp.com/

4.4.4. Update the user password

On the user home page, find the [Update password] (更新密码) button and click it to update your password.
